# Custom Tea Crop Mod

This mod adds a custom tea crop to Hytale.

## Features
- Tea seeds that can be planted on soil
- Tea crop with 3 growth stages
- Harvested tea leaves (crafting ingredient)
- Recipe to craft tea seeds

## Installation
1. Copy the CustomCrop folder to your Hytale mods directory
2. Launch Hytale

## Crafting
- Tea Seeds: 3x Life Essence at a Farming Bench (Tier 2)

## Growing
- Plant tea seeds on soil
- Wait for the crop to grow through 3 stages
- Harvest at the final stage to get tea leaves and life essence

## Assets
This mod currently uses wheat assets as placeholders. The wheat models and textures have been copied from the vanilla game assets.

### Included Assets
- Icons: Wheat icons are used as placeholders for tea items
- Models: Wheat crop models (Dense_Small_3Rows, Dense_Medium_3Rows, Dense_Tall)
- Textures: Wheat textures from the vanilla game

You can replace these with custom tea-themed assets by creating new models and textures in the Common folder.
