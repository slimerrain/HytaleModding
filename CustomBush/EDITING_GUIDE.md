## 🌸 Camellia Flower Reference

**Real Camellia Characteristics:**
- 5-7 rounded, overlapping petals
- Pink, white, or red colors (suggest pink/white for contrast with berries)
- Yellow stamens in center
- Glossy, dark green leaves
- Flowers bloom directly on stems

**Texture Tips:**
- Keep Hytale's slightly stylized art style
- Use soft pink (#FFB3C6) to light pink (#FFC9D9) colors
- Add subtle shading for depth
- Keep similar level of detail to berry textures
