# Quick Start Guide - Custom Camellia Bush Mod

## What This Mod Does

Adds **Camellia Bushes** to Hytale - beautiful flowering bushes that:
- 🌱 Can be planted and grown on soil
- 🌸 Produce harvestable pink camellia flowers
- 🗺️ Spawn naturally in plains and forest biomes
- ♻️ Regrow flowers after harvesting

## Installation

1. The mod is located at: `CustomBush/`
2. Copy this entire folder to your Hytale mods directory
3. Launch Hytale

## How to Use

### Finding Camellia Bushes
- Explore plains and forest biomes (Zone 1)
- Look for bushes with pink flowers growing naturally
- They spawn near where vanilla berry bushes appear

### Planting Camellia Bushes
1. Obtain a Camellia Bush block (from breaking a growing bush or finding one)
2. Place it on grass, dirt, or other soil blocks
3. Wait for it to grow through 5 stages
4. Growth takes ~2-4 minutes for full maturity

### Harvesting Camellia Flowers
1. Wait until the bush reaches final stage (pink flowers visible)
2. Right-click (Use) the mature bush
3. Collect 2-4 camellia flowers
4. Bush regrows flowers in ~30-40 seconds

### What You Get
- **Camellia Flowers**: 2-4 per harvest (main yield)
- **Bonus Drops**: Chance for extra flowers, Life Essence, or Sticks
- **Breaking Bushes**: Get Fibre and Sticks
